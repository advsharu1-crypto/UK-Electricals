How to Choose the Right Electrician in Triplicane

Start with the job. A fault, a new installation and a renovation are priced and scoped in different ways. Then compare businesses on the same points.

  • Scope. What work is included, and what is not?
  • Materials. Who supplies them, and which brands or grades?
  • Access to the person. Can you speak to the person who will do the work?
  • Availability. Can they attend your address on the day you need?
  • Paperwork. Will you get the agreed scope and the bill in writing?

Our longer checklist is in how to choose a reliable electrician in Triplicane.

Electrician vs Electrical Contractor: What Is the Difference?

An electrician is a trained person who installs, repairs and maintains wiring and fittings. An electrical contractor is a business that takes on electrical projects, usually with several workers, materials and paperwork, and may employ electricians.

For a dead socket or a fan point, an electrician is usually enough. For wiring a new floor, a shop fit-out or a building with several meters, a contractor is more likely. Licensing rules in Tamil Nadu depend on the type and size of the work, so ask which licence or permit applies to your job. You can read more in residential vs commercial electrical contractors or on our electrical contractor services page.

What Should You Check Before Booking an Electrician?

  • Quotation. Ask for a written quote that lists the work, the materials and any exclusions. Compare quotes on what they include, not only the total.
  • Materials. Ask which cables, switches and breakers will be used and whether they carry a BIS mark.
  • Work scope. Agree what happens if the electrician finds more work once a wall or board is opened.
  • Availability. Confirm the day, the time window and whether the person can reach your address.
  • Safety. The circuit should be switched off and isolated before work starts, and tested afterwards.
  • Payment. Be careful if most of the money is requested before any work begins.
