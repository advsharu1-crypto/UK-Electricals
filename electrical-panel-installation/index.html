Electrical Panel and Distribution Board Installation in Chennai

The distribution board is where your supply is shared out and protected. Our electrician installs, replaces and maintains distribution boards and electrical panels for homes and businesses in Chennai.

Call UK ElectricalsRequest an ElectricianWhatsApp Us

What this service covers

  • New distribution board installation
  • Replacement of old or damaged panels
  • Circuit protection devices arranged to suit the load
  • Panel layout and labelling
  • Panel maintenance and checks

Common problems customers describe

  • Old boards with rusted covers or loose connections
  • Not enough ways for the circuits you now need
  • Breakers do not match the load they protect
  • Unlabelled or messy panel wiring

When to get in touch

  • Your board is old, crowded or damaged
  • You are adding circuits and have no space left
  • Protection devices trip too often or not at all
  • A new building needs its main board installed

What you get from this service

  • A board sized and arranged for the circuits you actually use
  • Clear labelling so anyone can isolate a circuit quickly
  • Protective devices matched to the wiring they serve
Safety noteDistribution boards carry live parts even when a circuit is switched off. Leave panel work to a qualified electrician.

How Electrical Panel Installation Works

  1. Share the current setup

    Send a photo of the board and a list of heavy appliances.

  2. Plan the board

    We discuss capacity, circuits and protection.

  3. Installation

    The new board is fitted and circuits reconnected.

  4. Testing and labelling

    Circuits are tested and each breaker labelled.

Questions about electrical panel installation

When should a distribution board be replaced?

Visible damage, heat marks, no spare ways, or protection devices that no longer match the load are common reasons to ask for a review.

Do you label the circuits?

Clear circuit labelling is good practice. Ask for it when you book.

When should a distribution board be replaced?

Consider it if the board is old or damaged, has no spare ways, or you are adding heavy loads. An inspection can confirm.

Will the power be off during board work?

Yes, parts of the supply will be isolated while work is done. We will discuss how long and plan around your needs.
